## Why it worked (signals are weak: 9 comparable videos and no retention data)
- **Upload timing beats content.** The 25 Oct 19:50 batch got 3 hits out of 4. The 26 Oct 03:05 batch got 1 out of 4. Similar fights, close finishes and "Guess before the K.O.!" titles appear on both sides of the split, so the gap comes mostly from how YouTube tested each video, not from the fight itself.
- **Theme.** All three top videos star the spooky cast (Vampire, Necromancer, Wizard) or Knight. The holy and royal pair (Paladin vs King) flopped even in the strong batch.
- **Short and close.** Vampire vs Wizard had the closest finish (1 HP left), the most lead changes (5), the shortest fight and the best like rate. Archer vs Ninja (12 s) gained the most views between snapshots (+243). The long fights over 17 s did nothing.
- **Archer fatigue.** Five Archer videos were posted in 7 hours, four of them at the same moment. They probably competed with each other.
