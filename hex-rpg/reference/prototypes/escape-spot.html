prototype · not the game

Clues come in from mini‑games, searches and beaten monsters. Tap a hex to cross it off — again for a maybe, again to clear. When you think you know, arm Dig here and tap the spot. A wrong dig tells you how far off you were and costs a turn.

What you know

Tap a clue to hold it in mind. The app never crosses tiles off for you — that part is the game.

What this is testing. Whether crossing hexes off by tapping feels good on a tablet, whether four or five clues is the right amount of thinking for a ten‑year‑old, and whether a wrong dig telling you the distance keeps it fair rather than frustrating.

What is faked. Clues are all handed to you at the start; in the real game they arrive one at a time as you win mini‑games, search tiles and beat monsters. Nothing here is wired to the game — the map, the monsters and the puzzle are generated fresh each time you press New map.
